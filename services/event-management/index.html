How we work

Event and Guest Management

One operating plan joins the guest journey and the team behind it — invitations, RSVP, onsite flow and logistics — with fallbacks when timing, attendance or venue conditions change.

Map the journey

Define guest groups, invitation rules, approval paths, arrival peaks, VIP and general flow, venue constraints, and the information the operations team needs on the day.

Build & rehearse

Set up invitation, RSVP and guest-list processes, align onsite logistics and check-in flow, then rehearse with the client and venue team so exceptions are handled before doors open.

Operate live

Manage arrival, registration, escort and room flow; brief front-of-house; monitor attendance; and resolve last-minute changes without slowing the guest experience.

Close the loop

Reconcile attendance and guest data, deliver reporting, and capture what to improve for the next event.

Typical deliverables

Invitation plan and eDM, RSVP and guest-list management, onsite run-of-show and logistics briefing, QR check-in and floor flow, VIP / speaker handling, live attendance tracking, and post-event reporting.
