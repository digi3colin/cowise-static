MGM — ‘Awakening’ Special Exhibition Opening

As part of Art Macao 2021, MGM opened its ‘Awakening’ Special Exhibition, which ran until 31 October 2021. The opening on 16 July 2021 combined the exhibition’s opening ceremony with a Greater Bay Area Art Symposium, and was broadcast live for media and art audiences who could not attend in person.

Cowise built the live broadcast mini-site in Chinese and English and ran it through the afternoon. A countdown led into the symposium and then the ceremony, with the player and messages changing for each part of the programme. Media enquiries and press-kit requests were handled from the same page. When the broadcast ended, the site switched to a thank-you message pointing visitors to the exhibition website.
