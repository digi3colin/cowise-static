Women Power Forum 2021 — Registration and Live Streaming

The second Women Power Forum, organised by the Hong Kong Federation of Women under the theme ‘Herald a New Chapter of Harmony and Growth for Our World City’, took place on 23 October 2021 at Shun Tak Centre in Hong Kong. With travel still restricted, many speakers joined remotely, and the forum was streamed live on its official website alongside the audience in the hall.

Cowise delivered the forum’s registration and live streaming. Guests could register either to attend in person or to watch online, in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese or English. Invitation eDMs went out in Chinese and English. On the day, the stream page offered the original audio plus simultaneous interpretation channels in English, Mandarin and Cantonese, with a countdown before the forum and a closing screen afterwards. Each channel was also recorded for the organiser to use after the event.

Expertise

Trilingual registration

Separate forms to attend in person or watch online, in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English, with consent options for EU guests and automatic confirmation emails.

Invitation eDMs

Chinese and English invitation emails sent to the organiser’s guest list, linking straight to registration.

Multi-language live streaming

One stream page with the original audio plus English, Mandarin and Cantonese interpretation channels for viewers to choose from, and a countdown and closing screen around the live programme.

Hybrid speaker feeds and recordings

Remote speakers were mixed with stage cameras in the broadcast, and every language channel was recorded in full HD for post-event use.
