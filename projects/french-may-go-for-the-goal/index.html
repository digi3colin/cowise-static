Le French May — Go for the Goal 2024

Go for the Goal was an evening of football and party for a good cause, held to support Le French May’s cultural missions. Hosted by French May, the night took place on 21 June 2024 at Faye, across the 25th and 26th floors of California Tower in Central, and was timed around the EURO 2024 group match between France and the Netherlands. It featured a French DJ set, three hours of free-flow drinks with a French May themed cocktail and complimentary tickets to the ‘Noir & Blanc — A Story of Photography’ exhibition for early buyers.

Cowise built and ran the RSVP and ticketing platform behind the event. Guests could buy individual standing admission or book a VIP table by floor and size, from high tables for four on the rooftop to the VIP area behind the DJ booth. Payment was taken online, and every ticket was issued with its own QR code for entry on the night. The same desk handled guest enquiries, complimentary invitations and the door list.

Expertise

Ticketing storefront

A branded ticket shop for the event, built on Shopify with the campaign key visual, event details, ticket options, terms and inclement-weather arrangements on one page.

VIP table booking

Table packages organised by floor (indoor 25/F and rooftop 26/F) and table size, each with its own capacity and price, so guests could pick a table directly instead of booking through the RSVP desk.

Secure online payment

Tickets were paid for at checkout by card, with orders, payouts and refunds reconciled from the store for the organiser.

QR-coded admission

Each ticket was bound to a unique QR code for fast door check-in, and the guest list combined paid tickets with complimentary RSVPs.
