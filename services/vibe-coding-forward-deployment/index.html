How we work

Our methodology

The speed comes from shortening the loop between the person with the problem and the person writing the code — not from skipping the parts that keep software alive after launch.

Sit with the problem

Days one and two are spent with the team doing the work: the spreadsheet everyone dreads, the process that breaks at month end, the idea nobody has had time to test.

Build in the open

A working prototype within the first week, shown early and often. AI-assisted development makes rewrites cheap, so we change direction on evidence rather than defending an estimate.

Harden what survives

Once a prototype earns its place we give it what production needs — real authentication, data handling, error paths, tests around the parts that matter, and integration with the systems you already run.

Deploy & hand over

Shipped to your infrastructure with documentation, a runbook, and time set aside to train whoever inherits it. The engagement ends with your team able to change the thing without us.

Typical deliverables

Discovery notes and opportunity shortlist, clickable prototypes, an internal tool or MVP in production, integrations with existing CMS, CRM, e-commerce and event systems, workflow automation, source code and documentation, deployment and handover, plus optional retained support.

When to call us

Good fits

An idea nobody can cost

You need to know whether something is worth building before committing a budget to it. A prototype answers that faster than a proposal.

Work done by hand

Reporting, data reconciliation, guest lists, content updates — recurring manual work that a small internal tool would end permanently.

Systems that do not talk

The CMS, the shop, the CRM and the event platform each hold part of the picture and someone copies between them.

A team without capacity

Your developers are committed elsewhere and something needs building now, by someone who will work inside your stack rather than beside it.
