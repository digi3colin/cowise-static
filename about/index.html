About Us

COWISE IDEA LAB LTD., formerly Digi3 Studio, is a digital design agency that has been helping companies to take care of design, technology, data & business. Based in Hong Kong with team members of different backgrounds having over 20 years of experiences in the field to produce creative design and innovative solutions for engaging existing customers and acquiring new consumers for clients, old and new alike.
