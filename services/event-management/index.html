How we work

Our methodology

One operating plan joins the guest experience and the team behind it, with fallbacks for the moments when connectivity, timing, or attendance changes.

Map the journey

Define guest groups, invitation rules, approval paths, arrival peaks, venue constraints, and the data the team needs.

Build & rehearse

Create the RSVP, ticketing, communication and check-in tools, then test real devices and edge cases with venue staff.

Operate live

Prepare lists and hardware, brief the front-of-house team, monitor attendance, and solve exceptions without slowing the queue.

Close the loop

Reconcile attendance, campaign and interaction data, deliver reporting, and document improvements for the next event.

Typical deliverables

Registration sites, invitation and reminder eDM, QR tickets, guest-list tools, check-in kiosks, SMS, interactive installations, live dashboards, exports, and post-event reporting.
